# Changelog

## v2.0 (2026-10-10)

- New inputs: marketplace links (reads most of the inside straight from the page), GitHub links (plain public reads, never runs or downloads anything), and pasted template text.
- Creator mode: check your own template before you share it. Finds keys (named by type and place, never repeated), private links, personal facts, things that break for a stranger, and routines that would act on a stranger's account. Ends with "Ready to share" or "Fix these first."
- "Where it comes from" on every verdict: xAI team, community marketplace, a share link from elsewhere, or a GitHub project.
- "Words on the page": quotes first, then explains.
- Three choices after the outside verdict: interview the inside, outside only, or pause.
- New flags: planted "you already agreed" memories, crypto wallets the job does not need, bots that quietly drive logged-in tools on the shared computer, setup steps after install, lookalike copies, "come back daily" pushes.
- Money logins (bank, card, brokerage, crypto) kept signed in on the shared computer always open the verdict with a warning.
- After install: turn off every routine, run each by hand once while you watch, never press "Always allow" in a new bot's first days.
- Change tag is now a fingerprint of the exact words. Thirteen rules.
- Tested on a marketplace template (caught a routine it creates after install), a share link with a recording (caught a schedule that fires about 11 times a month, not once), a poisoned template (six red flags, did not obey), creator mode, a clean repo, and Homebrew's install script (yellow with eyes open, not red).

## v1.0 (2026-09-16)

- First public version. x.ai/bot share links only.
- Five steps: sniff the page (outside verdict), sniff the inside (screen recording first, screenshots as fallback), sniff the author, three questions against your existing bots, final verdict.
- Four verdicts. Twelve published rules. Memory limited to name, date, verdict, change tag.
- Rule of judgment: hidden is red, announced and fitting the job is yellow with eyes open.
- Never presses the final Add Bot button for you, and never asks you to press it just to inspect.
- Tested before launch on a poisoned template (caught it), a security bot that lists secret file names on purpose (yellow, not blocked), and two ordinary templates by recording and by screenshots.
